Requirements to start a thesis/project/internship
All members of the group must satisfy the criteria below individually. Furthermore, the student must register for Pre-Thesis 1 via a Google Form sent by the department. If one fails, he/she will not be allowed to join the group.
**Effective for groups registering from Fall 2022.
Criteria
	Requirement
	Credit
	Minimum: 75
	CGPA below 2.0
	Not eligible to start thesis/project/internship
	CGPA equal to or above 2.0
	Eligible to do a project/internship
	CGPA equal to or above 3.0
	Eligible to do a thesis/project/internship
	Number of group members
	Minimum: 1; Maximum: 5
**Internship: Maximum 1
	Additionally, for internship: If a student chooses an internship, s/he should apply to the Chairperson of the CSE department. The department will have a list of approved companies. In special cases, a student may suggest a different company for approval by providing a detailed company profile of the intended internship company/organization. An internal faculty member will be assigned as an internship supervisor/co-supervisor for the student.
________________
General Policies
1. A maximum of two supervisors and two co-supervisors is allowed for a thesis/project/ internship group
2. Final thesis/project/intern students are allowed to take a maximum of 3 courses in addition to the thesis/project (CSE 400). In exceptional cases, a petition form may be submitted to take more courses.
3. Pre-thesis I and Pre-thesis II must be completed before appearing at a thesis defense
4. Supervisors must send a list of thesis/project/internship groups they approve for final defense two weeks before the report submission deadline.
5. If any thesis/project/internship group fails to convince the examiners of their presentation or demonstration in any of the three phases, i.e., pre-thesis-I, pre-thesis-II, and final defense, that phase will be considered to be incomplete for that group. Supervisor's marks of an incomplete thesis will not be sufficient to obtain a grade for CSE400
6. Every thesis group must submit a camera-ready IEEE journal paper accompanying their final thesis report. 
7. If any thesis/project/internship group/individual fails to convince the examiners of their presentation or demonstration in any of the three phases, i.e. Pre-Thesis-I, Pre-Thesis-II, and Final Defense, that phase will be considered to be incomplete for that group/individual. Supervisor's marks or approval of the final report from the thesis committee of an incomplete thesis will not be sufficient to obtain a grade for CSE400.
8. Thesis / Project: Each level of the thesis (Pre-Thesis 1, Pre-Thesis 2, and Final Thesis) has a period of 1 semester
9. Internship: Internship Guidelines
