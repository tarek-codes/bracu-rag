# CSE Undergraduate Programs

## Department Vision
The vision of the CSE Department is to inspire and engage students to be creative, industry-ready critical problem solvers, and produce world-class researchers and entrepreneurs who will create jobs and solutions to build a prosperous Bangladesh.

## Institutional Core Values
- Proud, global institution from Bangladesh
- Bangladesh's flagship university
- Fostering Knowledge Creation
- Upholding Human Values
- Promoting Sustainable Development

## Mission Statements (M1 - M3)
- **M1:** To prepare individuals with professional competency who will be able to contribute to technological advancement.
- **M2:** To develop graduates with state-of-the-art multidisciplinary and collaborative research experience in the field of computer science and engineering.
- **M3:** To produce graduates enriched with human values and leadership skills.

## Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)
- **PEO1:** Prove professional competency with effective computer science and engineering solutions to real-life problems.
- **PEO2:** Demonstrate excellence in terms of knowledge, teamwork, and leadership skills while maintaining the highest level of ethical standards.
- **PEO3:** Engage in sustainable development of society through entrepreneurial and research skills.
- **PEO4:** Enrich knowledge, skills, and professional growth through lifelong learning, higher education, and professional training.
