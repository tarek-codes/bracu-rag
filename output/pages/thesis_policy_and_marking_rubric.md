# Part 1: Undergraduate Thesis & Capstone Policy

## 1. Eligibility & Requirements to Start

To initiate an undergraduate **Thesis**, **Project**, or **Internship** (CSE 400), each individual member of a group must independently fulfill all of the eligibility criteria outlined below. 

Furthermore, every student must register for **Pre-Thesis 1** through the official Google Form released by the CSE Department. If an individual fails to meet the criteria, they will **not** be permitted to join or remain in the group.

### Eligibility Criteria Table

| Parameter | Requirement | Status / Eligibility Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Completed Credits** | **Minimum 75 Credits** | Must be earned before registering for Pre-Thesis 1. |
| **CGPA < 2.00** | — | **Not eligible** to start a thesis, project, or internship. |
| **CGPA 2.00 – 2.99** | — | **Eligible for Project or Internship only** *(Not eligible for Thesis)*. |
| **CGPA ≥ 3.00** | — | **Eligible for Thesis, Project, or Internship**. |
| **Group Size (Thesis / Project)** | **1 to 5 Members** | Minimum: 1 student; Maximum: 5 students. |
| **Group Size (Internship)** | **Exactly 1 Member** | Maximum: 1 student (individual only). |

### Additional Regulations for Internship
* If a student selects the internship route, they must formally apply to the **Chairperson of the CSE Department**.
* The department maintains an official list of approved host companies/organizations.
* **Special Cases:** A student may propose an external company not on the pre-approved list by submitting a detailed company profile for formal departmental review and approval.
* An internal faculty member from the CSE Department will be formally designated as the student's **internship supervisor / co-supervisor**.

---

## 2. General Policies & Academic Regulations

1. **Supervision Quota:** A maximum of **two (2) supervisors** and **two (2) co-supervisors** is permitted for any single thesis, project, or internship group.
2. **Course Load Limit:** Students in their final thesis/project/internship phase are restricted to taking a maximum of **three (3) academic courses** in addition to CSE 400 (Thesis/Project). In exceptional scenarios, an official petition form must be submitted to request an overload.
3. **Sequential Progression:** Both **Pre-Thesis 1** and **Pre-Thesis 2** must be successfully completed and passed before a student or group is permitted to appear at the **Final Thesis Defense**.
4. **Supervisor Clearance Deadline:** Supervisors must formally submit the list of thesis/project/internship groups they approve for the final defense at least **two (2) weeks prior** to the official report submission deadline.
5. **Presentation & Demonstration Standard:** If any group or individual fails to convince the examiners during presentations or demonstrations in any of the three distinct phases (Pre-Thesis 1, Pre-Thesis 2, or Final Defense), that specific phase will be deemed **Incomplete**.
6. **Supervisor Marks Limitation:** A supervisor's marks alone—or approval from the thesis committee on an incomplete report—**will not be sufficient** to obtain a passing grade for CSE 400 if a phase remains incomplete.
7. **IEEE Journal Paper Mandate:** Every thesis group is strictly required to produce and submit a **camera-ready IEEE journal paper** alongside their final thesis report.
8. **Phase Timeline:** Each distinct level of the capstone sequence (**Pre-Thesis 1**, **Pre-Thesis 2**, and **Final Thesis**) spans a minimum duration of **one (1) semester** (constituting a 3-semester cycle).
9. **Internship Guidelines:** Students undertaking an internship must adhere to the standalone Departmental Internship Guidelines in addition to general CSE 400 requirements.

---

## 3. Evaluation, Promotion & Report Standards

### Promotion Threshold
* To be promoted to the subsequent phase (from Pre-Thesis 1 to Pre-Thesis 2, and from Pre-Thesis 2 to Final Defense), a group must achieve **at least 60% marks** in their respective phase evaluation.

### Report Submission & Plagiarism Standards
* **Plagiarism Threshold:** All submitted reports (Pre-Thesis 1, Pre-Thesis 2, Final Report) must exhibit a Turnitin similarity index of **≤ 15%**.
* **Turnitin Authentication:** Similarity checks must be conducted through official institutional channels (contact your supervisor or email `librarian@bracu.ac.bd`).
* **AI Detection Report:** Groups are required to submit the official **AI generated report** produced by Turnitin alongside their similarity index report.
* **Deadline Compliance:** All reports must be uploaded and submitted strictly within the announced deadlines for the active semester.

## 4. Penalties & Mark Deduction Schedule

Actions that breach policy or disrupt administrative coordination incur mandatory mark deductions. **These penalties are deducted from the overall grade and impact all members of the group:**

| Infraction Category | Mark Deduction | Conditions / Details |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Breakup / Division of a Group during Pre-Thesis 1** | **2 Marks** | Deducted from all involved group members. |
| **Breakup / Division of a Group during Pre-Thesis 2** | **3 Marks** | Deducted from all involved group members. |
| **Breakup / Division of a Group during Final Defense** | **5 Marks** | Deducted from all involved group members. |
| **Adding New Members after Pre-Thesis 1 Registration** | **2 Marks** | May be waived only under exceptional, approved circumstances. |
| **Requesting Information Update or Deferral after Deadline** | **1 Mark per change** | Imposed for each retroactive administrative modification. |
| **Requesting Thesis Title / Abstract Modification** | **0 Marks (1st time)**<br>**1 Mark per change (afterward)** | First title/abstract update is free; subsequent requests incur deductions. |
| **Mismatch between Database Title/Abstract & Submitted Report** | **2 Marks** | Applied when the final report metadata does not align with departmental records. |
| **Unexcused Absence in Final Defense (without formal deferral)** | **5 Marks** | Imposed on absent candidates who failed to submit deferral petitions. |
| **Unexcused Absence in Pre-Thesis 1 / Pre-Thesis 2 (without formal deferral)** | **2 Marks** | Imposed on absent candidates who failed to submit deferral petitions. |

# Part 2: OBE Marking Rubrics & Assessment Matrix

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering evaluates CSE 400 under an **Outcome-Based Education (OBE)** assessment model aligned with 14 Course Outcomes (COs) and ABET/BAETE Program Outcomes (POs).

## 5. Master Assessment Distribution (100 Marks)

The 100 total marks for CSE 400 are distributed across four evaluation bodies and milestones:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        CSE 400: TOTAL 100 MARKS                        │
├──────────────────┬──────────────────┬──────────────────┬───────────────┤
│  Pre-Thesis 1    │  Pre-Thesis 2    │  Defense Panel   │  Supervisor   │
│     (P1)         │     (P2)         │  (Final Defense) │  (Continuous) │
│    5 Marks       │    10 Marks      │    30 Marks      │   55 Marks    │
│     (5%)         │     (10%)        │     (30%)        │    (55%)      │
└──────────────────┴──────────────────┴──────────────────┴───────────────┘
```

| Evaluator / Milestone | Marks Allocated | Percentage | Primary Deliverables & Focus |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Pre-Thesis 1 (P1)** | **5.0** | 5% | Problem formulation, background literature review, methodology in brief (Chapter 1 & 2). |
| **Pre-Thesis 2 (P2)** | **10.0** | 10% | Preliminary design specifications, model architecture, methodology, and Poster Presentation (Chapters 3 & 4). |
| **Defense Panel** | **30.0** | 30% | Final oral defense, technical demonstration, performance evaluation, and overall report defense. |
| **Supervisor(s)** | **55.0** | 55% | Continuous semester evaluation, final report verification, teamwork, ethics, resource management, and IEEE paper. |
| **Total** | **100.0** | **100%** | Comprehensive capstone assessment. |

## 7. Stage-by-Stage Evaluation Breakdown

### 7.1 Pre-Thesis 1 (P1) Evaluation — 5 Marks
Pre-Thesis 1 represents the initial ideation, literature investigation, and formalization phase:
* **CO1 (2.5 Marks):** Formulation of problem statement, background, motivation, scope, and initial methodology (Chapter 1).
* **CO9 (2.5 Marks):** Quality of preliminary literature review, exploration of state-of-the-art research, and foundational concepts (Chapter 2).
* **Requirement to Advance:** Group must achieve a **minimum of 60% (3.0 / 5.0)** in P1 to register for Pre-Thesis 2.

### 7.2 Pre-Thesis 2 (P2) Evaluation — 10 Marks
Pre-Thesis 2 represents design specification, preliminary architecture, and mid-point dissemination:
* **CO5 (5.0 Marks):** Design process, system architecture, theoretical proofs, and preliminary modeling specifications (Chapter 4).
* **CO14 (5.0 Marks):** **Pre-Thesis 2 Poster Presentation**, visual design, technical communication, and verbal articulation during the department poster session.
* **Requirement to Advance:** Group must achieve a **minimum of 60% (6.0 / 10.0)** in P2 to be cleared for the Final Defense phase.

### 7.3 Defense Panel Evaluation — 30 Marks
The Final Defense Panel comprises designated faculty examiners who evaluate the final presentation, live demonstration, and final thesis document:
* **CO5 (10.0 Marks):** Evaluation of proposed engineering/theoretical solutions, model validity, and design rigor within constraints.
* **CO6 (5.0 Marks):** Assessment of performance analysis, benchmark comparisons, statistical validation, and optimization against alternatives.
* **CO8 (5.0 Marks):** Evaluation of contemporary IT/engineering tools utilized during the live presentation and demonstration.
* **CO14 (10.0 Marks):** Evaluation of the final written thesis report structure, oral defense quality, and student responses to panel queries.

### 7.4 Supervisor & Co-Supervisor Evaluation — 55 Marks
Supervisors provide continuous mentoring and comprehensive grading of the student's execution across the full lifecycle:
* **CO1 (2.5 Marks):** Depth and refinement of the final problem formulation.
* **CO2 (5.0 Marks):** Rigor in meeting final requirements, engineering constraints, and professional standards (Chapter 3.1).
* **CO6 (5.0 Marks):** Evaluation of performance results, comparative discussion, and analytical conclusions in the final report (Chapter 5).
* **CO7 (5.0 Marks):** Execution of final design adjustments, bug fixes, model tuning, and completed deliverable quality.
* **CO8 (5.0 Marks):** Tool proficiency, technical competence, and coding/experimental pipeline quality.
* **CO9 (2.5 Marks):** Independent learning capacity, adaptation to new concepts, and breadth of research synthesis.
* **CO10 (3.0 Marks):** Project management planning, milestones, timeline adherence, and risk mitigation strategies (Chapter 3.6 & 3.7).
* **CO11 (2.0 Marks):** Resource economic analysis and cost-benefit feasibility study (Chapter 3.8).
* **CO12 (5.0 Marks):** Academic integrity, ethical considerations, citation standards, and professional responsibility (Chapter 3.4).
* **CO13 (5.0 Marks):** Teamwork dynamics, equal contribution, peer accountability, and supervisor meeting attendance.
* **CO14 (5.0 Marks):** Quality and camera-readiness of the mandatory **IEEE journal paper format** accompanying the thesis.
