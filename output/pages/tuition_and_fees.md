### 1.4 Freshman Course Offerings & Subsequent Course Fees

#### Placement Courses
On the basis of admission test results, a student will be assigned to take one or more of the following freshman foundation courses:
1. **ENG 101** (Credit course)
2. **ENG 102** (Credit course)
3. **ENG 091** (Non-credit course)
4. **MAT 091** (Non-credit course)
5. **MAT 092** (Non-credit course)

#### Course Load & Fee Calculation Rules
* The exact number of course(s) a student is required to take is indicated against their name in the published admission test results.
* The initial admission fee package includes the tuition fee for **one (1) course**.
* Any additional course taken, up to a maximum load of **four (4) courses in total**, will incur subsequent course fees:
  * **All programs except Law:** **BDT 24,750/=** for each subsequent 3-credit course.
  * **School of Law (LLB):** **BDT 26,400/=** for each subsequent 3-credit course.
* **Special Rule for Non-Credit Courses:** If a freshman is prescribed two (02) non-credit courses, taking a third course in their first semester is **optional**.

#### Per-Credit Rate Summary
* **B.Arch Studio Fee:** BDT 8,800/= per credit
* **LLB Tuition Fee:** BDT 8,800/= per credit
* **Tuition Fee (All Other Programs):** BDT 8,250/= per credit

## 7. Total Study Cost & Degree Completion Estimates by Program

This section provides the comprehensive total study and degree completion cost for major undergraduate and postgraduate academic programs at BRAC University based on the latest fee structure (effective for new students from **Spring 2025 onwards**).

### 7.1 Cost Calculation Methodology & Standard Assumptions

The total estimated cost for degree completion accounts for all mandatory academic, operational, residential, and administrative fees required to graduate:

1. **Fixed One-Time Admission & Registration Fees:**
   * Admission Application Fee: **BDT 1,500/=**
   * Admission Fee (One-Time): **BDT 33,900/=**
   * Library Membership Fee (One-Time): **BDT 2,500/=**
   * *Subtotal Fixed Initial Overhead:* **BDT 37,900/=**

2. **Mandatory Residential Semester (RS) Fee:**
   * Residential Semester Fee: **BDT 88,000/=** (Mandatory for all undergraduate programs; completed during the 2nd year at Savar Residential Campus; effective Spring 2025 onwards).

3. **Mandatory Degree Processing & Career Readiness Fees:**
   * Degree Processing Fee: **BDT 8,500/=** (Payable prior to graduation/convocation).
   * Professional Skill Development Program (PSDP): **BDT 5,500/=** (Mandatory pre-graduation training).
   * *Subtotal Mandatory Graduation Fees:* **BDT 14,000/=**
   * *(Note: Convocation gown fee of BDT 3,500/= is omitted here as it is fully refundable upon return of the gown).*

> **Total Fixed Institutional Overhead Across All Programs:**  
> **BDT 37,900** (Initial) + **BDT 88,000** (RS) + **BDT 14,000** (Graduation) = **BDT 139,900/=**

4. **Program-Specific Variable Components:**
   * **Total Tuition Fee:** Calculated as `Total Required Credits × Tuition Fee per Credit` (BDT 8,250/= for standard programs; BDT 8,800/= for Law and B.Arch studio courses).
   * **Total Semester Fees:** Calculated as `Standard Number of Semesters × Fee per Semester` (BDT 12,600/= for CSE and ARC; BDT 14,000/= for Pharmacy and Law; BDT 9,350/= for other programs).

### 7.4 Variable & Incidental Considerations

1. **Non-Credit Freshman Remedial Courses:**
   Students required to take remedial courses based on admission test performance (ENG 091, MAT 091, MAT 092) pay **BDT 24,750/=** for each subsequent course taken in their first semester.
2. **Course Retakes and Incomplete Semesters:**
   Any retaken course incurs regular per-credit tuition (e.g., BDT 24,750/= for a 3-credit course, or BDT 26,400/= for Law). Extending degree duration beyond the standard number of semesters incurs the per-semester fee (BDT 9,350 to BDT 14,000) for each additional semester.
3. **Scholarships and Tuition Waivers:**
   BRAC University awards substantial merit-based scholarships, financial aid, sibling discounts, and special waivers (ranging from 10% up to 100% of tuition fees), which can significantly reduce these total degree completion estimates.
4. **Refundable Deposit:**
   At the time of convocation, a gown security deposit of **BDT 3,500/=** is collected, which is fully refunded upon return of the gown.
