# BRACU CSE

The vision of the CSE Department is to inspire and engage students to be creative, industry-ready critical problem solvers, and produce world-class researchers and entrepreneurs who will create jobs and solutions to build a prosperous Bangladesh.
| M1 | To prepare individuals with professional competency who will be able to contribute to technological advancement. |
| M2 | To develop graduates with the state-of-the-art multidisciplinary and collaborative research experience in the field of computer science and engineering. |
| M3 | To produce graduates enriched with human values and leadership skills. |
| PEO1 | Prove professional competency with effective computer science and engineering solutions to real-life problems. |
| PEO2 | Demonstrate their excellence in terms of knowledge, teamwork and leadership skills while maintaining the highest level of ethical standards. |
| PEO3 | Engage themselves in the sustainable development of the society through their entrepreneurial and research skills. |
| PEO4 | Enrich their knowledge, skill and professional growth through lifelong learning, higher education and professional training. |

| PO1 | Apply knowledge of mathematics, natural science, computer science and engineering fundamentals and specialization to the solution of complex computer science and engineering problems | |
| PO2 | Identify, formulate, research literature and analyze complex computer science and engineering problems reaching substantiated conclusions using first principles of mathematics, natural sciences and engineering and computing sciences. | |
| PO3 | Design solutions for complex computer science and engineering problems and design systems, components or processes that meet specified needs with appropriate consideration for public health and safety, cultural, societal, and environmental considerations. | |
| PO4 | Conduct investigations of complex problems using research-based knowledge and research methods including design of experiments, analysis and interpretation of data, and synthesis of information to provide valid conclusions. | |
| PO5 | Create, select and apply appropriate techniques, resources, and modern engineering and IT tools, including prediction and modeling, to complex computer science and engineering problems, with an understanding of the limitations. | |
| PO6 | Apply reasoning informed by contextual knowledge to assess societal, health, safety, legal and cultural issues and the consequent responsibilities relevant to professional engineering practice and solutions to complex computer science and engineering problems. | |
| PO7 | Understand and evaluate the sustainability and impact of professional engineering work in the solution of complex computer science and engineering problems in societal and environmental contexts. | |
| PO8 | Apply ethical principles and commit to professional ethics and responsibilities and norms of engineering and computing practice. | |
| PO9 | Function effectively as an individual, and as a member or leader in diverse teams in multi-disciplinary settings to create a collaborative and inclusive environment, establish goals, plan tasks, and meet objectives | |
| PO10 | Communicate effectively on complex activities with the computer science and engineering community and with society at large, such as being able to comprehend and write effective reports and design documentation, make effective presentations, and give and receive clear instructions. | |
| PO11 | Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of engineering management principles and economic decision-making and apply these to one’s own work, as a member and leader in a team, to manage projects and in multidisciplinary environments. | |
| PO12 | Recognize the need for, and have the preparation and ability to engage in independent and life-long learning in the broadest context of technological change. |
