# Inter-Department Transfer Form

## Overview & General Instructions
- **Form Name:** Inter-Department Transfer Form (Department Change Form)
- **Form Reference:** Department Change Form/01/12/2012
- **Issuing Authority:** Office of the Registrar, BRAC University
- **Required Attachments:** Attach copies of Grade Sheets of all completed semesters and provide the list of courses you want to transfer on the reverse side of this form.

## Required Student Information
- **Name of the Student**
- **Present Student ID**
- **Present Department & Present Program**
- **Proposed Department & Proposed Program**
- **Reason for Seeking Transfer**
- **Current CGPA on Completion of Credits**
- **Student Signature & Date**
- **Previous Record of Inter-Department Transfer:** Yes / No (If Yes, provide Old Department/Program and Old ID)

## Academic Eligibility & Minimum Subject Requirements for Transfer
To qualify for transfer into the following programs, students must have completed the required subjects in HSC or A-Level results:

| Destination Department / Program | Required Subjects in HSC / A-Level Results |
|---|---|
| Computer Science (CS), Mathematics | Mathematics |
| Electrical & Electronic Engineering (EEE), Electronic & Communication Engineering (ECE), Computer Science & Engineering (CSE), Applied Physics & Electronics (APE), Physics | Physics & Mathematics |
| Biotechnology, Microbiology, Pharmacy | Biology & Chemistry |

## Departmental Approval Workflow
1. **Chairperson, Accepting Department:** Approved / Not Approved, Signature, Date, and Department Seal.
2. **Chairperson, Releasing Department:** Approved / Not Approved, Signature, Date, and Department Seal.

## Retained Course List & Credit Transfer
Students indicate courses they wish to retain for onward CGPA computation after transfer to the new department (noting that some courses may not contribute toward degree requirements of the new department):

| Course Code | Course Title | Credits | Grade | Chairperson / Course Coordinator Approval (Accepting Dept.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Clearance Fees & Final Registrar Processing
- **Accounts Clearance:** Please deposit a total of **Tk. 5,000** (Authorized Signature, Name, and Date required).
- **Office of the Registrar & Controller of Examinations:**
  - Assigns New Student ID Number
  - Records Total Credits Transferred
  - Records Current CGPA
  - Records Department and Program designation
  - Official Signature of the Office of the Controller of Examinations, Date, and Seal
