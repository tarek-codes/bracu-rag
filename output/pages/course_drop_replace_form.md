# Course Drop & Replace Form

## Overview & Submission Guidelines
- **Form Name:** Course Drop / Replace Form
- **Form Reference:** Course Drop Form/01/04/2014
- **Issuing Authority:** Office of the Registrar, BRAC University
- **Submission Destination:** Please submit the completed and approved form directly to the Registrar's Office.

## Required Student Information
- **Student Full Name**
- **Student ID**
- **Department / School**
- **Current CGPA**
- **Current Semester:** Spring / Summer / Fall (Year)
- **Contact Details:** Address, Telephone Number, Mobile Number, and Email Address

## Course Information Required
To drop or replace a course, students must provide:
- **Course Code**
- **Course Title**
- **Semester Taken**
- **Current Grade**
- **Reason for Dropping or Replacing the Course**
- **Additional Student Comments** (if any)

## Departmental Approvals & Signatures
1. **Student Signature & Date:** Certifies the student request.
2. **Department Chairperson / Dean Approval:**
   - Decision: YES (Approved) or NO (Rejected)
   - Chairperson / Dean's Comments
   - Chairperson / Dean's Signature, Date, and Official Seal
