# BRACU CSE

The vision of the CSE Department is to inspire and engage students to be creative, industry-ready critical problem solvers, and produce world-class researchers and entrepreneurs who will create jobs and solutions to build a prosperous Bangladesh.
- Proud, global institution from Bangladesh
- Bangladesh's flagship university
- Fostering Knowledge Creation
- Upholding Human Values
- Promoting Sustainable Development
M1 | To prepare individuals with professional competency who will be able to contribute to technological advancement. |
M2 | To develop graduates with the state-of-the-art multidisciplinary and collaborative research experience in the field of computer science and engineering. |
M3 | To produce graduates enriched with human values and leadership skills. |
PE01 | Prove professional competency with effective computer science and engineering solutions to real-life problems. |
PE02 | Demonstrate their excellence in terms of knowledge, teamwork and leadership skills while maintaining the highest level of ethical standards. |
PE03 | Engage themselves in the sustainable development of the society through their entrepreneurial and research skills. |
PE04 | Enrich their knowledge, skill and professional growth through lifelong learning, higher education and professional training. |
