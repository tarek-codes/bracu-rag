# CSE Advising & Registration Guidelines

## Freshers & First-Year Advising
- **First Semester Course Load:** Freshers typically take 3 or 4 courses out of: CSE110, MAT092, MAT110, ENG091, ENG101, ENG102, PHY111 as assigned by the Admissions Office.
  - Taking more courses enables faster graduation and early progression to advanced study or career.
  - Taking fewer courses helps maintain a higher initial CGPA.
- **Advisor Designation:** Please note that a **Department Advisor** is distinct from a **FYAT (First Year Advising Team) Advisor**.
- **Class Schedules:**
  - Class routines and seat allotments are published on [USIS](http://usis.bracu.ac.bd).
  - Theory classes commence from the official semester start date.
  - Laboratory classes commence from the first Saturday following the first week of classes.

## Course Sequencing & 12 Curriculum Variations
- The department provides 12 distinct suggested curriculum flow variations (6 for CS and 6 for CSE) depending on the initial courses assigned in the first semester.
- General Education (GenEd) requirements must be fulfilled from the approved university GenEd Streams.

## Advising Policies, Clash Resolution & Credit Limits
- **Conflict Resolution (Clashes):** Students must NOT take any course with examination, theory, or laboratory time clashes. All clashes must be resolved through remote registration adjustments. No clash will be approved without explicit written consent from the course coordinator or instructor.
- **Credit Limit Overload:** Students requesting to take credits above the standard semester ceiling must submit a justifiable reason to their advisor or email the Department Chairperson with detailed academic rationale.
- **Academic Probation Advising:** Students with a CGPA below 1.50 are on academic probation and must consult designated probation advisors before registering for any courses.

## Department Chairperson Approvals & Formal Petitions
- For requests requiring Chairperson approval, students should email `chair.cse@bracu.ac.bd` with all supporting documentation and academic transcripts attached.
- To request additional section seats, students must contact the individual faculty member teaching that section, who submits seat requests through the department.

## Emergency Student Services & Academic Procedures
- **Semester Drop (During Registration Week):** Drop all enrolled courses with the advisor so the course list is blank, then complete the Semester Drop Form from the Department Coordination Office (DCO).
- **Semester Drop (After Registration Week):** Review the Semester Drop Policy, complete the Semester Drop Form, and submit it to the DCO accompanied by detailed medical or emergency documentation.
- **Course Drop / Replacement:** To drop a non-compulsory course from the grade sheet to improve CGPA, submit the official Course Drop / Replacement Form.
- **Inter-Department / Program Change:**
  - Compare completed coursework with the curriculum roadmap.
  - Complete the Inter-Department Transfer Form listing all completed and ongoing ('I' grade) courses.
  - Submit the form to the DCO, obtain signatures from both chairpersons, and retain a photocopy for personal records before submission to the Registrar.

## USIS Grading Troubleshooting
- Ensure all accounts and financial dues are fully cleared; grades remain blocked or display incomplete ('I' / 'F') status until all installment dues are cleared.
- Students on approved installment plans can request the exam office to publish their results after the final installment is processed.
- Students may proceed with prerequisite course registration while grade clearing is underway.

## Undergraduate Thesis, Project & Internship (CSE400) Guidelines
- **Coordinators:** Dr. Md. Golam Rabiul Alam and Mr. Moin Mostakim.
- **Eligibility:** Students are eligible to initiate **Pre-thesis 1 (P1)** upon completing **75 credits**.
- **Structure:** The capstone thesis sequence spans 3 consecutive semesters:
  1. **Pre-Thesis 1 (P1):** Proposal and initial literature review.
  2. **Pre-Thesis 2 (P2):** Methodology, experimental setup, and mid-progress review.
  3. **Final Thesis Semester (CSE400):** Final defense, report submission, and presentation.
- **Enrollment Rule:** Register formally for CSE400 on USIS only once, during the final defense semester.
