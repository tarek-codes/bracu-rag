# CSE Student Forms

## Examination Forms
- **Make-up Examination Form:** Used by students who missed midterm or final examinations due to documented medical or family emergencies.

## Course Registration & Academic Council Forms
- **Course Drop / Replace Form:** For dropping an elective or non-compulsory course or replacing it with an equivalent course.
- **Department / Program Change Form (Inter-Department Transfer Form):** For transferring into or out of the CSE Department.
- **Admission Cancellation Form:** For students requesting formal withdrawal and admission cancellation.
- **Document Request Form:** For requesting official departmental certificates, verification letters, or syllabi copies.
- **Semester Drop Form:** For taking a formal leave of absence / dropping an entire semester during or after registration week.
- **Semester / Course Drop with 'W' Grade:** For withdrawing from a course after the add/drop deadline with a 'W' recorded on transcript.
- **Multiple Fail Academic Council Appeal Form:** For students seeking Academic Council clearance after multiple course retakes.
- **Semester Gap Form:** For requesting authorized leave spanning one or more consecutive semesters.
- **Time Extension Form:** For undergraduate students requiring extension beyond standard study time limits.
- **CS Minor Application Form:** For non-CSE students seeking to pursue a formal Minor in Computer Science.
